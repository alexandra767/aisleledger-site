Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

AisleLedger (the "App", on iPhone and Android) keeps your shopping data on your phone. There are no accounts. The only things that leave your phone are described below: barcode lookups, Kroger price checks, and anonymous usage statistics you can turn off.

What stays on your phone

Your stores, tax settings, trips, scanned items, prices and price history are stored only on your phone. If you delete the App, that data is deleted with it.

Phone backups

Your phone's own backup (iCloud on iPhone, Google's backup on Android) may include the App's data, stored under your Apple or Google account like your other apps' data. We never see it. On Android, receipt and coupon photos are left out of that backup.

Camera

The camera reads barcodes, shelf-tag prices, coupons and receipts. Images are processed on the device and are never uploaded. A coupon photo you choose to keep is saved on your phone with that coupon, so you can show it to the cashier.

On Android, this reading uses Google's ML Kit, which runs on the phone. ML Kit sends Google anonymous diagnostic data to keep it working — device and app information, performance and error counts, and an identifier for this install that isn't meant to identify you or the phone. Google says it does not pass this data to third parties (Google's ML Kit data disclosure).

Location

If you allow it, your location is used once when you start a trip, to suggest the nearest store you've saved. A store's location is saved on your phone with that store. Your location is never sent to us or anyone else.

Product names (Open Food Facts)

When you scan an item the App hasn't seen before, it sends only the barcode number to Open Food Facts, a free public food database, to look up the product's name. Nothing that identifies you is sent with it.

Kroger prices

If you link a saved store to a Kroger-family store, scanning an item there sends the barcode number and that Kroger store's ID to our price server, which asks Kroger's public product service for the price. Finding a Kroger store sends the ZIP code you type, or the location saved with that store (the server rounds it to about a kilometer before looking it up). Nothing that identifies you is sent. The server keeps prices for a few hours so repeat scans are fast; it does not keep a record of who asked. It runs on Cloudflare.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which features get used, the App sends anonymous usage statistics to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-first analytics service based in Germany: event names (for example "trip started" or "coupon saved"), counts such as how many items a trip had, the app and operating-system version, device model, locale, and a one-way hash of an anonymous identifier for this install of the App (on iPhone, the vendor identifier) that cannot be turned back into you. It never includes prices, totals, item or store names, barcodes, coupon details, or anything you type. You can turn it off any time in Settings → Privacy → Share anonymous usage statistics.

Crash reports

If the App crashes or freezes, the phone gives it a technical summary the next time it opens (the kind of error and where in the App's code it happened) — from Apple on iPhone, from Android's own crash and freeze records on Android. With usage statistics on, the App sends that summary to TelemetryDeck so we can fix it. It contains no shopping data, photos or anything you typed, and the same switch turns it off. On Android, Google Play's own crash reporting, which your phone's usage and diagnostics setting controls, also shows us crashes, without anything that identifies you.

Receipts

Receipt photos you scan, and the text read from them, are saved on your phone with that trip and never uploaded.

Ads and tracking

The App contains no advertising software, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Purchases

AisleLedger Pro is an auto-renewing subscription handled entirely by Apple (App Store) on iPhone or Google (Google Play) on Android. We never see your payment details. Their privacy policies cover the purchase: apple.com/legal/privacy and policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
