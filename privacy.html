Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

AisleLedger (the "App") keeps your shopping data on your iPhone. There are no accounts, and we run no servers that receive your data.

What stays on your phone

Your stores, tax settings, trips, scanned items, prices and price history are stored only on your iPhone. If you delete the App, that data is deleted with it.

Camera

The camera reads barcodes and shelf-tag prices. Images are processed on the device and are never uploaded or saved.

Location

If you allow it, your location is used once when you start a trip, to suggest the nearest store you've saved. A store's location is saved on your phone with that store. Your location is never sent to us or anyone else.

Product names (Open Food Facts)

When you scan an item the App hasn't seen before, it sends only the barcode number to Open Food Facts, a free public food database, to look up the product's name. Nothing that identifies you is sent with it.

Analytics, ads and tracking

The App contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Purchases

AisleLedger Pro is an auto-renewing subscription handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy covers the purchase: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
