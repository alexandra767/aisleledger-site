Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

AisleLedger (the "App") keeps your shopping data on your iPhone. There are no accounts. The only things that leave your phone are described below: barcode lookups, Kroger price checks, and anonymous usage statistics you can turn off.

What stays on your phone

Your stores, tax settings, trips, scanned items, prices and price history are stored only on your iPhone. If you delete the App, that data is deleted with it.

Camera

The camera reads barcodes, shelf-tag prices and coupons. Images are processed on the device and are never uploaded. A coupon photo you choose to keep is saved on your phone with that coupon, so you can show it to the cashier.

Location

If you allow it, your location is used once when you start a trip, to suggest the nearest store you've saved. A store's location is saved on your phone with that store. Your location is never sent to us or anyone else.

Product names (Open Food Facts)

When you scan an item the App hasn't seen before, it sends only the barcode number to Open Food Facts, a free public food database, to look up the product's name. Nothing that identifies you is sent with it.

Kroger prices

If you link a saved store to a Kroger-family store, scanning an item there sends the barcode number and that Kroger store's ID to our price server, which asks Kroger's public product service for the price. Finding a Kroger store sends the ZIP code you type, or your saved store's location rounded to about a kilometer. Nothing that identifies you is sent. The server keeps prices for a few hours so repeat scans are fast; it does not keep a record of who asked. It runs on Cloudflare.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which features get used, the App sends anonymous usage statistics to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-first analytics service based in Germany: event names (for example "trip started" or "coupon saved"), counts such as how many items a trip had, the app and iOS version, device model, locale, and a one-way hash of your device's vendor identifier that cannot be turned back into you. It never includes prices, totals, item or store names, barcodes, coupon details, or anything you type. You can turn it off any time in Settings → Privacy → Share anonymous usage statistics.

Ads and tracking

The App contains no advertising or crash-reporting software, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Purchases

AisleLedger Pro is an auto-renewing subscription handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy covers the purchase: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
