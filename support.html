Support

Email support@gigledger.org. Put "AisleLedger" in the subject so we can find it fast. We read every message. Your app version and phone model are filled in for you; nothing about your shopping is included.

The same help is in the app: Settings → Help & Support (iPhone and Android).

Quick start

  1. Add your store. Tap Start shopping at your store. The first time, tap Add your store: pick how it taxes — sales tax, commissary surcharge or no tax — and its rate, and saving it starts your first trip there.
  2. Scan as you shop. Scan the item's barcode — it goes straight into your cart, with its name and picture when AisleLedger knows them. Items you've bought here before get their price automatically; others say price? until the shelf tag is read, you type it, or the receipt fills it in.
  3. Watch the total. The running total at the top of the cart includes tax, sales and your coupons, so the register holds no surprises.
  4. Check out. Tap Checkout. Scan the receipt or type its total, then Finish trip. Trips shows what you spent and saved.

How it works

Stores and tax

Each store keeps its own tax setting. Many states tax groceries at a lower rate: set the Grocery rate in the store's settings, then tap the item and pick Taxed as to mark it Grocery, Standard or No tax. Commissaries add a surcharge instead of tax.

Scanning items and prices

Point the camera at the barcode and the item lands in your cart. If this store already knows its price, it's priced on the spot. If not, it says price? — hold the camera on the shelf tag for a moment, and the price it reads shows on the strip under the camera — with the name printed on the tag, if the item has none yet: tap Use $X to take it (the name comes too), Not this if it's the wrong number, or Type price. Later, tap the item and Read the shelf tag to try again. Sale tags are caught too, and what you saved is counted. Scanned the wrong thing? Tap Undo on the banner, or Undo last add in the ⋯ menu. The cart list sits under the camera with a running total. In your cart (tap Done to put the camera away), tap an item's price to fix it, or tap the item for its name, quantity, tax, aisle, a coupon, or to remove it.

Price book

Every price you scan is saved by store. Open Price book from Shop to see the latest price at each store and the history. Next time you scan that item, the last price here shows up, and you're asked to check before a price far from it is saved. When the last price was a sale that has since ended, the item waits for today's price and offers last time $X (sale) as a one-tap choice; it stays marked as a sale so it isn't remembered as the regular price.

Budget

In the cart, open the ⋯ menu and tap Set a budget. A bar under the total fills as you shop, turning orange near the cap and red past it. Change budget is in the same menu, and Remove budget is inside it.

Coupons

Open Coupons from Shop and tap Scan a coupon to read its barcode, or take a photo and AisleLedger fills in the amount and expiry. You can also type one in. Coupons come off the right item automatically as you scan, and Expiring soon reminds you before they run out. A coupon for the whole order — "$5 off $50" or "10% off your order" — is a coupon too: pick The whole order when you add it, with its minimum if it has one. It joins the trip by itself once your cart qualifies, counts once after any item coupons, and shows already checked at checkout.

Kroger prices

In a store's settings, tap Link to a Kroger store and pick yours. Scans there send the barcode and your Kroger store's number to our price server, which asks Kroger for its own price and sale and keeps no record of who asked. Finding your store uses the ZIP code you type or the store's saved location, rounded to about a kilometer; nothing that identifies you is sent.

Deals

After you scan, tap Deals on the banner — or tap the item and tap Deals for this item — to open your store's own website search for that product and check its sale price. In the price book, Deals at a store does the same. Works at any store.

Receipts

At checkout, tap Scan receipt (or Choose a receipt photo). AisleLedger reads the total into the box, keeps the photo with the trip, and checks the receipt line by line. Forgot? Open the trip in Trips and tap Add the receipt. Shopped without scanning? On Trips, tap the receipt button at the top and Add a receipt so it still counts toward what you spent.

Receipt check

Rang up $3.49 — your price was $2.99 means the register charged more than the price you scanned: check the shelf tag, and customer service can usually fix it. Rang up 3 — you scanned 2 means it was rung up more times than you scanned. Couldn't match lists items it couldn't pair up — receipts shorten names, so it won't guess — plus anything on the receipt you didn't scan, with its price. Nothing there is counted as an overcharge.

Trips

Trips shows what you spent and saved this month, how many trips you made, and how close your estimates were to the receipts. Tap any trip to see its items, receipt and check. To correct a receipt total the scan misread, open the trip and tap its Receipt row. An item that still says price? can be priced there too, and a price you added after the trip can be tapped to fix it. Delete a trip from its page, or from the cart's ⋯ menu; the coupons it used go back in your wallet.

Export my data

Settings → Export my data saves everything you've entered as a JSON file and your price book as a spreadsheet (CSV), to keep or move elsewhere. Receipt and coupon photos stay on the phone.

Privacy

Your stores, trips, prices, coupons and receipt photos stay on this phone. Product pictures are downloaded from Open Food Facts for the barcodes you scan and kept on this phone; nothing about you goes with the request. Anonymous usage statistics — which features get used, and a count when something failed quietly (a save, a price lookup) by kind and area, never what was in it — and crash summaries help fix and improve the app; turn them off in Settings → Privacy. The app also keeps a short list of its own last steps on this phone, for support emails; Settings → Privacy clears it.

Subscription

Free for 7 days

Use everything free for 7 days — no card needed. After that, AisleLedger Pro is a monthly or yearly subscription; the subscription screen shows the price in your currency. Your data stays on the phone either way.

Restore purchases (on iPhone)

On the subscription screen, tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

Cancel (on iPhone)

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then AisleLedger.

Restore purchases (on Android)

On the subscription screen, tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same Google account you subscribed with.

Cancel (on Android)

Open the Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions, then AisleLedger.

Questions

My total is off from the receipt

Check the store's tax setting first. Many states tax groceries at a lower rate, so set the grocery rate in the store's settings. Then tap each item and pick Taxed as: Grocery, Standard or No tax. After a few receipts, AisleLedger suggests a better rate.

It read the wrong price

Tap the item's price and type the right one. AisleLedger remembers the price you typed for that store.

An item's name looks wrong

Tap the item and type a better name in the box at the top. It sticks for next time.

Where do item names come from?

From a free product database, when it knows the barcode. If it doesn't, the item says Unnamed item until it gets a name: from the shelf tag (the strip shows the tag's name with its price, and Use $X keeps both), from your receipt (Walmart prints each item's number, so the name beside it is used — receipts without numbers name nothing), or one you type. Tags and receipts shorten names the way the store prints them, like GV COLA 10OZ. Whatever name an item gets is remembered for every trip after, and a name you typed is never replaced.

An item says "price?"

This store doesn't know that item's price yet, so it is in your cart but not in the total. Hold the camera on the shelf tag and tap Use $X when the price shows; or tap price? and type it; or just keep shopping — when you scan the receipt at checkout, the receipt's price fills it in. Items still without a price are left out of the estimate and are not compared to the receipt.

Removing an item from the cart (on iPhone)

Swipe the item's row to the left and tap Remove (or tap the item and choose Remove from cart). A strip at the bottom offers Undo for 5 seconds; after that it's gone. Removing a row never deletes the item's price from your price book.

Removing an item from the cart (on Android)

Swipe the item's row to the left and it's removed (or tap the item and choose Remove from cart). A strip at the bottom offers Undo for 5 seconds; after that it's gone. Removing a row never deletes the item's price from your price book.

Where is my cart?

Shop always opens on your open cart. To shop somewhere else at the same time, open the ⋯ menu and tap Switch store; when more than one cart is open, each is listed there under Open trips, and a line under the total says which other cart is open.

The receipt total it read is wrong

Type the right total in the box — what you type always wins. If it is already saved, open the trip and tap its Receipt row to change it; when a later scan reads a different total, the app asks which to keep. Long or crumpled receipts read best with Scan receipt held flat, in good light.

The receipt didn't fill in my prices

AisleLedger fills a missing price from the receipt only when the lines it read add up to the receipt's own subtotal (and its items-sold count, when the receipt prints one). If a line was missed, or a price looked like it belonged to the line next to it, it fills nothing rather than guess, and says so. Retake the photo with the receipt flat and straight, in good light — or tap price? and type it.

Why does the receipt check say Couldn't match?

Receipts shorten names (KRO 2% MLK), so some items can't be matched for sure. AisleLedger only reports an overcharge when it's certain it found the same item.

The coupon scan says it's not a coupon

Scan the long barcode on the coupon (the one with stacked bars), not the item's barcode. If it still won't read, take a photo or type it in.

Kroger prices don't show up

Link the store first: open the store's settings and tap Link to a Kroger store. Kroger prices need an internet connection; without one, your own scanned prices are used.

Are my receipt and coupon photos uploaded?

No. Photos stay on your phone. Only anonymous counts — like how many receipts were saved — are shared if usage statistics are on.

Camera access is off (on iPhone)

Open the Settings app, tap Apps (or scroll down), then AisleLedger, and turn on Camera.

Camera access is off (on Android)

Open Settings → Apps → AisleLedger → Permissions, and allow Camera.

Will my data move to a new phone? (on iPhone)

If your iPhone backs up to iCloud, or you move to a new iPhone phone-to-phone, your stores, trips, prices and coupons come along. Settings → Export my data also saves a copy you can keep.

Will my data move to a new phone? (on Android)

If your phone backs up to Google, your stores, trips, prices and coupons come back when you set up a new phone with the same Google account; receipt and coupon photos don't (a direct phone-to-phone transfer brings those too). Settings → Export my data also saves a copy you can keep.

Can I delete a trip?

Yes. Open the trip and tap the trash can, or open the cart's ⋯ menu and tap Delete this trip. Its items, receipt and photo go with it, and any coupons it used are available again. There is no undo.

Can I delete a store? (on iPhone)

Yes, once nothing uses it. Open the cart's ⋯ menu and tap Switch store, then swipe the store's row to the left and tap Delete. If the store still has trips, prices or coupons, AisleLedger says which — delete those first.

Can I delete a store? (on Android)

Yes, once nothing uses it. Open the cart's ⋯ menu and tap Switch store, then tap the trash icon on the store's row. If the store still has trips, prices or coupons, AisleLedger says which — delete those first.

Where do I see my plan?

Settings → Subscription shows whether you are in the free week or a member, and the plans.

How do I get a refund? (on iPhone)

Apple handles payments. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with the Apple ID you subscribed with, find AisleLedger Pro and choose Request a refund. Apple decides and replies within a few days.

How do I get a refund? (on Android)

Google Play handles payments. Within 48 hours of a charge, open play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory, find AisleLedger Pro and choose Request a refund. After that, email us at support@gigledger.org and we will sort it out with Google.

How do I delete my data?

Everything is on your phone, with no account anywhere. Use Export my data in Settings first if you want a copy; deleting the app then removes all of it. Usage statistics are anonymous and cannot be traced back to you; turn them off in Settings → Privacy.

What does Email support include?

Your app version, phone model and system version are filled in; nothing about your shopping is. You can also add the app's recent activity — the screens it opened, what it did, and anything that failed, with no prices, items or stores in it — but only after you have read it and turned it on. Remove clears it from the phone.
