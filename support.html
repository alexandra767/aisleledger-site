Support

Email support@gigledger.org. Put "AisleLedger" in the subject so we can find it fast. We read every message. Your app version and phone model are filled in for you; nothing about your shopping is included.

The same help is in the app: Settings → Help & Support (iPhone and Android).

Quick start

  1. Add your store. On Shop, under Start a trip, tap New store. Pick how it taxes — sales tax, commissary surcharge or no tax — and its rate.
  2. Scan as you shop. Scan the item's barcode, then the shelf tag to read its price. Sale tags are caught too. Can't scan? Tap Or type price.
  3. Watch the total. The running total at the top of the cart includes tax, sales and your coupons, so the register holds no surprises.
  4. Check out. Tap Checkout. Scan the receipt or type its total, then Finish trip. Trips shows what you spent and saved.

How it works

Stores and tax

Each store keeps its own tax setting. Many states tax groceries at a lower rate: set the Grocery rate in the store's settings, then use Taxed as on an item's row to mark it Grocery, Standard or Exempt. Commissaries add a surcharge instead of tax.

Scanning items and prices

Scan the barcode to name the item, then the shelf tag to read the price. If a tag shows a sale and a regular price, AisleLedger keeps both and counts what you saved. Change the quantity with Qty, or tap an item to fix its price.

Price book

Every price you scan is saved by store. Open Price book from Shop to see the latest price at each store and the history. Next time you scan that item, the last price here shows up, and you're warned if the new price looks off.

Budget

Under Start a trip, type a Budget cap before you pick the store. A bar under the total fills as you shop, turning orange near the cap and red past it.

Coupons

Open Coupons from Shop and tap Scan a coupon to read its barcode, or take a photo and AisleLedger fills in the amount and expiry. You can also type one in. Coupons come off the right item automatically as you scan, and Expiring soon reminds you before they run out.

Kroger prices

In a store's settings, tap Link to a Kroger store and pick yours. Scans there fill in Kroger's own price and sale. Finding your store uses the ZIP code you type or the store's saved location, rounded to about a kilometer; nothing that identifies you is sent.

Deals

After you scan, tap Deals on the banner — or press and hold an item and tap Deals for this item — to open your store's own website search for that product and check its sale price. In the price book, Deals at a store does the same. Works at any store.

Receipts

At checkout, tap Scan receipt (or Choose a receipt photo). AisleLedger reads the total into the box, keeps the photo with the trip, and checks the receipt line by line. Forgot? Open the trip in Trips and tap Add the receipt. Shopped without scanning? On Trips, tap the receipt button at the top and Add a receipt so it still counts toward what you spent.

Receipt check

Rang up $3.49 — your price was $2.99 means the register charged more than the price you scanned: check the shelf tag, and customer service can usually fix it. Rang up 3 — you scanned 2 means it was rung up more times than you scanned. Couldn't match lists items it couldn't pair up — receipts shorten names, so it won't guess — plus anything on the receipt you didn't scan, with its price. Nothing there is counted as an overcharge.

Trips

Trips shows what you spent and saved this month, how many trips you made, and how close your estimates were to the receipts. Tap any trip to see its items, receipt and check.

Export my data

Settings → Export my data saves everything you've entered as a JSON file and your price book as a spreadsheet (CSV), to keep or move elsewhere. Receipt and coupon photos stay on the phone.

Privacy

Your stores, trips, prices, coupons and receipt photos stay on this phone. Anonymous usage statistics (which features get used) and crash summaries help fix and improve the app; turn them off in Settings → Privacy.

Subscription

Free for 7 days

Use everything free for 7 days — no card needed. Then AisleLedger Pro is $4.99 a month or $29.99 a year. Your data stays on the phone either way.

Restore purchases (on iPhone)

On the subscription screen, tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

Cancel (on iPhone)

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then AisleLedger.

Restore purchases (on Android)

On the subscription screen, tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same Google account you subscribed with.

Cancel (on Android)

Open the Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions, then AisleLedger.

Questions

My total is off from the receipt

Check the store's tax setting first. Many states tax groceries at a lower rate, so set the grocery rate in the store's settings. Then mark each item as Grocery, Standard or Exempt with Taxed as on its row. After a few receipts, AisleLedger suggests a better rate.

It read the wrong price

Tap the item and type the right price. AisleLedger remembers the price you typed for that store.

An item's name looks wrong

Tap the name while you're adding the item and type a better one. It sticks for next time.

The receipt total it read is wrong

Type the right total in the box — what you type always wins. Long or crumpled receipts read best with Scan receipt held flat, in good light.

Why does the receipt check say Couldn't match?

Receipts shorten names (KRO 2% MLK), so some items can't be matched for sure. AisleLedger only reports an overcharge when it's certain it found the same item.

The coupon scan says it's not a coupon

Scan the long barcode on the coupon (the one with stacked bars), not the item's barcode. If it still won't read, take a photo or type it in.

Kroger prices don't show up

Link the store first: open the store's settings and tap Link to a Kroger store. Kroger prices need an internet connection; without one, your own scanned prices are used.

Are my receipt and coupon photos uploaded?

No. Photos stay on your phone. Only anonymous counts — like how many receipts were saved — are shared if usage statistics are on.

Camera access is off (on iPhone)

Open the Settings app, tap Apps (or scroll down), then AisleLedger, and turn on Camera.

Camera access is off (on Android)

Open Settings → Apps → AisleLedger → Permissions, and allow Camera.

Will my data move to a new phone? (on iPhone)

Your data lives only on this phone. Settings → Export my data saves a copy you can keep; moving it into a new phone isn't supported yet.

Will my data move to a new phone? (on Android)

If your phone backs up to Google, your stores, trips, prices and coupons come back when you set up a new phone with the same Google account; receipt and coupon photos don't (a direct phone-to-phone transfer brings those too). Settings → Export my data also saves a copy you can keep.
