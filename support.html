Support

Email support@gigledger.org. Put "AisleLedger" in the subject so we can find it fast.

My total is off from the receipt

Check the store's tax setting first. Many states tax groceries at a lower rate, so set the grocery rate in the store's settings. Then mark each item as Grocery, Standard or Exempt with "Taxed as" on its row.

It read the wrong price

Tap the item and type the right price. AisleLedger remembers the price you typed for that store.

An item's name looks wrong

Tap the name while you're adding the item and type a better one. It sticks for next time.

Restore my subscription

On the subscription screen, tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

Cancel

Open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then AisleLedger.
