Totals are estimates

AisleLedger estimates your total from the prices you capture and the tax settings you choose. Shelf tags, sale rules, coupons and tax rules vary, and a camera can misread a price. The amount your store charges at the register is what you owe. Check your receipt.

Subscription

AisleLedger includes a 7-day free trial. After it ends, AisleLedger Pro is $4.99 per month or $29.99 per year. Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless you turn it off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel it in your Apple ID settings, under Subscriptions.
