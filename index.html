AisleLedger

Scan items as you shop and always know your total, including tax, before you reach the register.

  • Scan the barcode, then the shelf tag. The price is read on your phone.
  • Your running total includes sales tax. Grocery rates, tax-free stores and the commissary surcharge are all supported.
  • A personal price book remembers what things cost at each store, so the second trip is instant.
  • Sale prices are flagged, with what you're saving.
